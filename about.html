About

Ethereal is an independent digital publication exploring softness, restraint, and the space between image and meaning.

It exists outside trend cycles, capturing fragments of thought, visual experiments, and quiet editorial expressions.

This space is a pause, an archive, a quiet study of form — where content breathes and lingers, leaving room for reflection.

Ethereal is an independent digital magazine founded by Anastasia.
